About the Studio

Radii Office is a free suite of web apps that run right in your browser, with a glossy Frutiger Aero look and a few languages of their own.

What it is

Radii Office is a set of small, fast apps (writing, notes, slides, spreadsheets, drawing, 3D, email, chat and more) that all share one look and one idea: you can type short tags around your text and it turns into formatting. Your work is saved in your own browser, and every app can keep several documents side by side.

The ideas behind it

Keydown

The formatting language every app understands: chips, quotes, dividers, math, spoilers and hundreds of styles.

Read the guide →

BitLang

A small programming language for Bitbook pages, with buttons, effects and hover changes.

Plume

A component language and compiler: write HTML, CSS and a little code, and get a working piece of interface.
